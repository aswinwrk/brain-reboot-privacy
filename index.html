Privacy Policy

Brain Reboot — Memory Challenge Game

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Brain Reboot ("the App"), developed by Focus Spark ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Brain Reboot does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. The App does not require account creation, login, or registration.

All game data (scores, settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device only using standard device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

We display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free availability of the App. Google AdMob may collect and use data in accordance with Google's privacy policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location data (country/region level)
  • App usage data and interaction with ads
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)

For more details on how Google uses this data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Google Play Games Services

The App may use Google Play Games Services for leaderboard functionality. If you choose to sign in, your Google Play Games profile name and scores may be visible to other players on the leaderboard. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted:

  • Game progress (current level, sequence, streak)
  • High score and leaderboard records
  • Player display name (gamer tag)
  • App preferences (music, sound effects, haptics, theme settings)

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Children's Privacy

Brain Reboot is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertisements shown are served through Google AdMob's general audience settings.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, there is minimal risk to your personal information. All game data remains on your device. Third-party services (Google AdMob, Google Play Games) maintain their own security measures as described in their respective privacy policies.

7. Your Choices

  • Advertising ID: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • Game Data: You can clear all locally stored game data by clearing the app data or uninstalling the App.
  • Play Games: Sign-in to Google Play Games is optional. You can disconnect through your Google account settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
